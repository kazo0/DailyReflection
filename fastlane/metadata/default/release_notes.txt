Easier to read.

• The reading, the Sober Time count and the Settings rows are larger again, as they were before the rebuild.
• New Text Size setting (Settings → Readings): Normal, Large, Extra Large or Huge. It scales the reading and the Sober Time tab, the reading column grows with it, and your device's own text size setting still applies on top.
• Tab labels no longer run into each other at the largest accessibility text sizes.
