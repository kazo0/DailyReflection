Start each day with a reflection. Daily Reflection brings you the reading for today from Daily Reflections, the book of reflections written by A.A. members for A.A. members, with a reading for every day of the year.

Made for people in Alcoholics Anonymous and other 12 step recovery programs, it is a quiet, simple daily reader for your morning meditation, a meeting, or a moment of calm during the day.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Today's reading: open the app and the day's reflection is there. Browse to any other date of the year.
• Sober time counter: set your sobriety date and see how long you've been sober, in days or in years, months and days.
• Daily reminder: a notification at the time you choose, so you never miss a reading.
• Secular readings: prefer an agnostic approach? Turn on Secular Readings in Settings to read from Beyond Belief: Agnostic Musings for 12 Step Life instead.
• Share a reading with your sponsor, a sponsee or a friend.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works offline: every reading is on your phone.
• No account, no sign-up, no ads.
• No analytics or tracking. No data is collected or shared.

Whether you count days or decades of sobriety, Daily Reflection helps you keep the program at the center of each day. One day at a time.
